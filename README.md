 🎬 MindFlix — Intelligent Movie Suggestions by Mood

MindFlix is a smart movie recommendation web app that helps you discover movies based on your current mood or personal preferences. Whether you're feeling romantic, thrilled, nostalgic, or just bored — MindFlix finds the perfect movie for your vibe 🧠✨

---

 🔥 Features

- 💬 Get movie suggestions by simply describing your mood
- 🌍 Explore international cinema — not just Hollywood!
- ⭐ Filter by rating to find top-quality films
- 🎥 One-click access to trailers via YouTube
- ❤️ Save favorite movies locally with just a tap
- 💄 Clean, colorful, responsive UI

---

🚀 Live Site

👉 [https://mindflix.vercel.app](https://mindflix.vercel.app) *(or your custom domain)*
